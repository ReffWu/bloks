Use this when a conversation with a prospect or client has gone quiet, or after a meeting, and someone wants the follow-ups planned.

1. Read the thread and any notes. Write down what was last said, what was promised on both sides, and what they care about.
2. Plan at most three touches over the next few weeks. Each must give them something: an answer they asked for, a useful example, a clear next step. Never a message that only says you are following up.
3. Space them out; a week or more between touches unless they asked for speed.
4. Draft each one, short, in the person's own voice, with one clear ask.
5. Stop after the third. A fourth unanswered message hurts more than it helps.

Not for: a first cold message to someone new, or a reply to a message that just came in. Answer that message.
Return: the plan as dates and purposes, then each draft with a subject line.
Approval: always required before anything is sent. Never send or schedule messages on your own.
