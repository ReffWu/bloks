Use this when someone asks what to publish next, or for a plan covering the coming weeks.

1. Start from what is already happening: launches, events, seasons and anything published recently, so nothing repeats or clashes.
2. Choose two or three themes for the period, each tied to something the audience is trying to do.
3. For each piece give: the working title, the theme, the format (post, article, video, newsletter), the one point it makes, the owner, and the date.
4. Keep the plan to what the team can actually produce. Count the pieces against the people; cut rather than overfill.
5. Leave one slot a week open for something timely.

Not for: one post somebody needs today. Draft that post instead.
Return: a table by week with title, theme, format, owner and date, then one line on what was left out and why.
Approval: none needed to plan. Publishing anything needs approval.
