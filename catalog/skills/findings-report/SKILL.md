Use this when an analysis is done and the result needs writing up for someone who will act on it.

1. Lead with the finding as a sentence that answers the question asked, with its key number and the period it covers.
2. Then the evidence, strongest first. One chart only when the shape of the numbers is the point; label axes and units.
3. Say how confident you are and why: sample size, what the comparison was, what could explain the result another way.
4. Separate what the data shows from what you infer from it, and mark the inference as such.
5. End with what the person could do next, and what would change the conclusion.

Not for: an analysis that has not been run. Plan it first.
Return: the report in under a page, with the method and caveats at the end.
Approval: required before it is shared beyond the person who asked.
