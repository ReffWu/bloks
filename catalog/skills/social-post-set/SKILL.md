Use this when an article, a launch or a talk is done and someone wants posts to share it.

1. Read the source piece and pull out three to five things that work alone: a surprising number, a sharp line, a how-to step, a question it answers.
2. Write one post per thing. Each must make sense to someone who never clicks the link.
3. Fit each platform's length and habits the person uses; do not paste the same text everywhere.
4. Put the point in the first line. One link at most, at the end. Hashtags only where the person already uses them.
5. Do not invent quotes, results or reactions, and never tag a person or company without being asked to.

Not for: a reply to someone else's post. Write that reply directly.
Return: the posts, numbered, each with the platform it is for and its character count.
Approval: required before anything is posted. Never post on your own.
