Use this when a draft, a brief or a post makes factual claims and someone wants them checked before it is shared.

1. List every claim the piece depends on: numbers, dates, quotes, names, and anything stated as a fact about the world.
2. For each, find the primary source: the original report, filing, dataset, documentation or the person's own words. Commentary about a source is not the source.
3. Mark each claim: confirmed (with the source), needs a change (with the right version), or could not confirm.
4. Check quotes word for word and numbers digit for digit, including units and the year they refer to.
5. Flag claims that are true but misleading in context, and say why in one line.

Not for: a general research question with no draft yet. Research it instead.
Return: the claims that need a change first, each with the fix and its source, then the confirmed list, then what could not be confirmed.
Approval: none needed, this is read-only. Changes to the draft are the author's to approve.
