Use this when someone is planning a trip and wants the options laid out.

1. Pin down the fixed points first: dates, where they must be and when, who is going, and the budget. Ask for whichever is missing.
2. For travel and places to stay, give two or three real options each, with price, times, the cancellation terms and the trade off ("cheaper, but lands at midnight").
3. Build a day by day outline that respects travel time, opening hours and rest. Leave slack; a plan with no gaps fails on the first delay.
4. Note what needs doing before leaving: documents and their expiry dates, entry rules, vaccinations, things to reserve ahead.
5. Prices and availability change. Say when you checked.

Not for: one question about a place or a connection. Answer it.
Return: the fixed points, then the options with prices and terms, then the day by day outline, then the before-you-go list.
Approval: always required before booking, reserving or paying for anything, or sharing travel details with anyone.
