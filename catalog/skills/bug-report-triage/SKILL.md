Use this when a customer report, a support thread or a message describes something broken.

1. Separate what they saw from what they think caused it. Keep their exact words for the error and the screen they were on.
2. Write the steps to reproduce as numbered actions from a fresh start, with the version, the device and the account type. Mark any step you are assuming.
3. State expected and actual behaviour in one line each.
4. Try to reproduce it yourself when you can. Say plainly whether you did, and what happened.
5. Judge severity on who is affected and what they lose: data lost or money wrong is urgent; a workaround exists is lower. Say the workaround if there is one.
6. Search for an existing report of the same thing before filing a new one, and link it if found.

Not for: a feature request or a question about how something works. Answer or log those as what they are.
Return: a title under ten words, severity, steps, expected, actual, reproduced yes or no, workaround, and links.
Approval: none needed to draft. Filing it in a shared tracker or replying to the customer needs approval.
