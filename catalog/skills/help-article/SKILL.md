Use this when the same question keeps coming in, or a feature needs a page people can find on their own.

1. Title it with the question in the reader's words ("How do I change my billing email?"), not the feature's name.
2. Answer in the first two lines. Many readers stop there.
3. Then numbered steps, one action each, naming buttons and menus exactly as they appear on screen.
4. Add a short "If this does not work" section with the two likeliest reasons and what to do about each.
5. Follow the steps yourself on the current version before calling it done. Note the version you checked.
6. Link the article from the replies that prompted it, so the next person finds it.

Not for: a one-off answer to one customer. Write the reply instead.
Return: the article in plain Markdown, and a list of the support threads it answers.
Approval: required before it is published.
