Use this when a launch, a release or an announcement is about to go out and someone asks whether it is ready.

1. Write down what is launching, to whom, and when, in one line each. If any of the three is unknown, that is the first blocker.
2. Walk the list: the thing itself works for a new user from start to finish; the page, the email and the post say the same thing; prices, dates and links are right; support knows it is coming and has answers to the three likeliest questions; there is a way to roll back or pause.
3. Mark each item done, not done, or not checked. Never mark something done because someone said it was; open it and look.
4. For each item not done, name who owns it and the smallest thing that would close it.
5. End with a call: ready, ready with named risks, or not ready. One sentence on why.

Not for: planning a launch weeks out, or a question about one item on the list. Answer that item instead.
Return: the one line call first, then the blockers with owners, then the full list with its marks.
Approval: none needed to check. Required before changing a date, a price or anything public.
