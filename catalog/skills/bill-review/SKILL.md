Use this when someone wants their recurring charges looked over: subscriptions, utilities, insurance, memberships.

1. Work only from what the person has shared: statements, receipts, emails or a list. Do not log in anywhere to find more.
2. List each recurring charge with its amount, how often, and when it next renews.
3. Flag: a price that went up since last time, two services doing the same job, anything not used in the last two months as far as the records show, and anything renewing in the next thirty days.
4. For each flag, say what the person could do (keep, downgrade, cancel, ask for a better rate) and what they would save in a year.
5. Never decide for them. Leave the choice and the action with the person.

Not for: one bill somebody wants explained. Read it and explain it.
Return: the flagged charges first, each with its option and yearly saving, then the full list, then the renewals in date order.
Approval: always required before cancelling, changing or paying anything. Never contact a provider on your own.
