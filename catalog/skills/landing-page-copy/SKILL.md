Use this when someone needs the words for a product or launch page.

1. Before writing, get three answers: who exactly this is for, the one problem it solves for them, and the single action the page asks for. If you cannot get them, ask; do not guess an audience.
2. Headline: the outcome for that person, in under ten words. No slogans, no puns, no "the future of".
3. Under it, one sentence that says how, and the one call to action.
4. Then three sections at most, each a short heading and two or three lines: what it does, why it is different from what they use now, and what it costs or how to start.
5. Every claim has to be true today. Leave out anything planned but not shipped, and any comparison you cannot back up.
6. Read it aloud. Cut every word that does not help the person decide.

Not for: a single headline or button label. Offer three options for that instead.
Return: the page as headed sections in plain text, then two alternative headlines.
Approval: required before anything is published.
